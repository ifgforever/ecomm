Where to buy Labubu in Chicago without the queue

Most people hunting a Labubu in Chicago end up in one of two places: refreshing the Pop Mart site for a restock that sells out in seconds, or paying a reseller triple on a marketplace. There is a third option, and it is a lot less painful — buy one secondhand, in person, from a shop that already has it on the shelf.

That is what we do. Jojin’s Kitty is a thrift shop at 4100 N Pulaski Rd, and Labubu is one of the things we actively look for when we take items in. Figures, plush, keychains, bag charms, the occasional Pop Mart travel bag — whatever comes through, goes out on the floor.

What makes this different from a reseller

  • You can see it before you buy it. Every piece is secondhand, so condition matters. Pick it up, turn it over, check the feet and the fuzz. No listing photos to squint at.
  • Thrift-shop pricing. We price against what we paid, not against what the secondary market is doing that week.
  • No blind box roulette. You are choosing a specific one, not gambling on a series and ending up with your fourth duplicate.

Labubu is the fastest-moving thing in the shop. If you see one on this page, it is worth calling ahead before you drive over — the inventory below updates live, but a piece can walk out the door between a page load and a parking spot.

Getting here

We are on N Pulaski Rd just north of Irving Park Rd, in Old Irving Park on the Northwest Side — an easy trip from Portage Park, Avondale, Albany Park, Jefferson Park and Logan Square, and considerably less of a production than fighting for parking in Chinatown or on Michigan Ave. Street parking out front is usually fine.

Common questions

Where can you buy Labubu in Chicago?

Pop Mart operates stores and vending machines downtown and in Chinatown, but they sell out fast and stock is unpredictable. For secondhand Labubu, Jojin’s Kitty Thrift Shop at 4100 N Pulaski Rd carries figures, plush and keychains as they come in, Monday through Saturday, 10am to 7pm.

Do you sell real Pop Mart Labubu?

Everything we take in is secondhand, and we check pieces before they go on the floor. Because these are pre-owned, you are welcome to inspect anything in person before you buy — and we will tell you honestly what we know about where a piece came from.

How much does a Labubu cost at your shop?

It depends entirely on the piece and its condition. We price against what we paid rather than the resale market, so it is generally well under what the same figure goes for on a marketplace. Current prices are on the live inventory above.

Can you hold a Labubu for me?

Call or text the shop at (312) 610-0321. We are a small shop and we will do what we reasonably can, but Labubu moves quickly and we cannot promise a long hold.
