Where to find Monster High in Chicago

New Monster High is easy — any big-box toy aisle carries the current reboot line. The dolls people are actually hunting for are the original 2010s generation, and those only exist secondhand. Jojin’s Kitty is a thrift shop, and Monster High is one of the things we deliberately watch for when items come in.

What actually turns up

The Monster High shelf rotates constantly, but the mix usually looks like this:

  • Dolls — Draculaura, Frankie Stein, Clawdeen Wolf and Lagoona Blue most often, with rarer ghouls like Spectra, Operetta and Abbey Bominable appearing now and then
  • Playsets and furniture — beds, vanities and the occasional bigger piece
  • Accessories and fashion packs — shoes, stands, brushes, diaries and loose outfits
  • The odd grail — first-wave dolls and exclusives do come through; they never last the week

Because it is all secondhand, the Monster High here skews toward the original G1 run rather than the current reboot — exactly the era collectors and people who grew up with it are looking for.

Why a thrift shop is the better hunt

Original-generation Monster High was discontinued years ago, so the only way to get it is secondhand — and online prices have climbed accordingly. Digging through a thrift shelf is where the deals still are, and it is why this section is worth checking more than once. Stock turns over every week.
