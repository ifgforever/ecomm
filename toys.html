A Toy Store in Chicago That Isn't Like the Others

Not a big-box aisle of the same twelve things — a thrift shop where the toy section is vintage figures, dolls, plush and whatever came through the door this week.

Why bother with a secondhand toy store

Every toy store in Chicago that buys new stock is selling roughly the same catalogue. The thing you actually remember — the Fisher-Price Little People figure, the Lalaloopsy, the Cabbage Patch doll, the figure from a line that got cancelled in 2004 — is not in that catalogue. It only exists secondhand.

The toy section is the largest part of this shop by a wide margin, and it is genuinely unpredictable. Vintage figures and playsets, dolls, plush, Squishmallows, trading cards, and a steady stream of things that defy a category.

What the section covers

  • Vintage figures & playsets — Fisher-Price Little People turn up constantly, alongside older licensed figures
  • Plush — Squishmallows, character plush and a large Sanrio contingent
  • Dolls — Barbie, Cabbage Patch, Lalaloopsy and similar
  • Trading cards & collectibles — packs and singles as they come in

Toys are the fastest-turning section in the shop. If you are bringing kids, it is worth knowing that the floor genuinely looks different week to week — which is the reason people come back.

Prices are thrift prices

Nothing here is priced against a collector guide. A vintage figure costs what a secondhand vintage figure should cost, which is most of the appeal — you can let a kid actually play with it, and you can pick up something you have not seen in twenty years without thinking hard about it.

Visiting

4100 N Pulaski Rd, just north of Irving Park Rd on the Northwest Side, open Monday through Saturday, 10am to 7pm. Convenient from Portage Park, Old Irving Park, Albany Park, Avondale and Jefferson Park.

Common questions

Where are the best toy stores in Chicago for vintage toys?

For secondhand and vintage toys specifically, thrift shops beat new-stock retailers. Jojin’s Kitty Thrift Shop at 4100 N Pulaski Rd runs a large rotating toy section of vintage figures, dolls, plush and Funko Pop, open Monday through Saturday, 10am to 7pm.

Do you sell Funko Pop?

Yes — there is a standing Funko Pop shelf of around a hundred figures. It has its own page with the current stock listed.

Are the toys clean and in good condition?

Everything is secondhand and sold as-is. Items are looked over before they go on the floor, and condition is part of how a piece is priced. You can inspect anything in person before buying.

How often does new stock arrive?

Continuously — the toy section turns over faster than anything else in the shop, so the floor genuinely changes from one week to the next.
