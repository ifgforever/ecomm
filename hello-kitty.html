Is there a Sanrio store in Chicago?

Worth being straight about this, because it is the thing everyone searches: there is no official Sanrio flagship store in Chicago. You will find Sanrio sections inside larger retailers and a rotating amount of it at specialty shops, but a dedicated Sanrio store is not something the city has.

What Chicago does have is secondhand Sanrio, and a fair amount of it passes through here. Jojin’s Kitty is a thrift shop — the name is not an accident — and Hello Kitty and her Sanrio friends are one of the things we deliberately look for when items come in.

What actually turns up

The Sanrio corner is one of the largest single themes in the shop, and it is broader than just Hello Kitty:

  • Plush — Hello Kitty, My Melody, Kuromi, Cinnamoroll, Pochacco, Pompompurin, in every size from keychain to hug-sized
  • Wallets and coin purses — kiss-lock wallets and card cases turn up constantly
  • Bags, totes and storage — including the hard-shell Hello Kitty cases
  • Accessories — hair clips, bracelets, phone cases, stationery and the odd watch

Because it is all secondhand, the Sanrio here skews older than what is in stores now — late-90s and 2000s pieces show up regularly, which is exactly what people who grew up with it are actually looking for.

Why a thrift shop is the better hunt

New Sanrio is easy to buy and identical everywhere. The interesting pieces — discontinued characters, old packaging, the Kuromi thing you had in middle school — only exist secondhand. That is the whole appeal of digging through a thrift shop instead of adding to cart, and it is why the Sanrio shelf here is worth checking more than once. Stock turns over every week.

Finding us

4100 N Pulaski Rd, just north of Irving Park Rd in Old Irving Park. Easy to reach from Portage Park, Avondale, Albany Park and Logan Square, and street parking is usually available right out front.
