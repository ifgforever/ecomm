A Funko shelf that is priced like a thrift shop

Buying Pops in Chicago usually means one of two things: paying retail for whatever is currently on a big-box peg, or paying secondary-market prices for anything that is not. Neither is much fun if what you actually enjoy is digging.

Jojin’s Kitty is a thrift shop at 4100 N Pulaski Rd, and the Funko shelf is one of the larger single sections in it — around a hundred figures at any given time, taken in secondhand and priced against what we paid rather than against a sold-listings search.

What is usually on it

  • Marvel and DC — Venom, Harley Quinn, Superman, WandaVision, What If… and the rest of the deep bench
  • Stranger Things — a recurring run, including 8-Bit and convention pieces
  • Anime and games — Naruto, Demon Slayer, Pokémon, Fallout
  • Disney and classics — including VHS Covers and the Trains line
  • Retailer exclusives — Walmart, Walgreens and convention stickers turn up regularly

Prices on the shelf generally run from around $6 to $30. Because everything is secondhand, boxes vary — some are clean, some are shelf-worn, some figures are out of box entirely. It is marked either way, and you can look before you buy.

Worth checking more than once

There is no distributor and no reorder here — the shelf is whatever came through the door. That makes it unpredictable in the way that is actually the point: the exclusive you have been half-looking for is either sitting there this week or it is not. The live inventory below is the fastest way to check before making the trip, and calling ahead costs nothing.

Finding the shop

4100 N Pulaski Rd, just north of Irving Park Rd in Old Irving Park, Chicago 60641. An easy run from Portage Park, Avondale, Albany Park, Jefferson Park and Logan Square, with street parking usually free out front. Open Monday through Saturday, 11am to 4pm.
