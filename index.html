Jojin's Kitty Thrift

One woman's treasure hunt, open to everyone. Stop by, dig around, find something good.

📞 +1 (312) 610-0321

Jojin's been a bargain hunter her whole life — always hunting for a good find, always ready to pass one along. After a multi-day yard sale turned into something bigger than she expected, she decided to make it permanent. She's Filipina, born and raised with deep roots here in Chicago, and a mom of four. If you bring the kids, don't be surprised if she lights up — nothing makes her happier than seeing a kid smile over something they found here. — Welcome to the shop. Come dig around.
